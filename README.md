# Greetings! <img width="30" src="https://media.tenor.com/BLBESZVhftkAAAAi/pokemon-mudkip.gif" alt="Mudkipz" />
<!-- Source: https://tenor.com/view/pokemon-mudkip-mudkip-pokemon-pokemon-mudkip-gif-24760601 -->

I'm Edward Isaac T. Jumawan, a BS Computer Science student at the University of the Philippines Diliman with a passion for building technology that solves real-world problems.

I remember my very first project, it was a LAN Messenger built way back during the COVID-19 pandemic in 2020. I was looking for a way to connect with my family despite being quarantined. I couldn't rely on usual chat apps because we didn't have stable internet connection (looking back, I still don't know why). I didn't know how to make apps back then, but that didn't stop me from trying. And, eventually, I made it, using Python and Tkinter.

Ever since then, I've been fascinated by the endless possibilities of technology and how it can be used to solve real-world problems. I've worked on a wide range of projects, including embedded systems, full-stack web development, game development, machine learning, and AI applications.

Ironically, one of the things I look forward to most is finding problems. Every challenge is an opportunity to learn something new, build something useful, or rethink an old assumption. I enjoy breaking down complex issues and searching for solutions, but I also find just as much satisfaction in seeing how other people solve them. Whether I'm creating my own projects or learning from others, that process of turning obstacles into opportunities is what drives me to continue learning and growing as a problem-solver.
